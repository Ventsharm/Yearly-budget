About your reminders

Subscriptions, payments and cancellations are one-time reminders. Paid or completed items stay in date order. Unfinished reminders stay on your list after the date passes. Edit any reminder to set a new date. Day-before alerts are checked daily around 9:30 AM UK time. The check can run late; reminders added or moved after it may miss their alert.
